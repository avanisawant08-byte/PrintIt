## 1. What is Print It?

**Print It** is an online platform that connects everyday people who need document printing or stationery items with local print shops.

Instead of standing in long lines at a print shop, handing over USB flash drives, or emailing files back and forth, users can:
- Upload documents from their smartphone.
- Choose exact print settings (color or black-and-white, page sizes, paper type, single or double-sided, binding style).
- See the exact price instantly before paying.
- Track their order in real time while the shop prints it.
- Walk in to pick up the printed documents or get them delivered to their doorstep.

Print shop owners get a clean digital dashboard that notifies them of incoming print jobs, organizes their print queue, manages stationery sales, and handles payments automatically.

## 3. Everyday Features Explained Simply

### For Customers (Mobile App)

- **Easy Sign-In**: Create an account with your email or log in instantly using your Google account.
- **Find Nearby Shops**: View a list of local print shops near you, see if they are currently open or closed, read their services (e.g., color printing, photo printing, spiral binding), and check their location.
- **Upload & Custom Print Setup**:
  - Select PDFs or photos from your phone.
  - Choose **Color** (Black & White vs Full Color).
  - Choose **Paper Size** (Standard A4, Large A3, etc.).
  - Choose **Sides** (Print on one side or both sides of the paper).
  - Choose **Pages** (Print all pages or specific page numbers like pages 1 to 5).
  - Choose **Copies** (How many physical sets you want).
  - Choose **Binding** (Staple, Spiral binding, Book cover, or no binding).
  - Choose **Delivery Method** (Self-pickup at shop or doorstep delivery).
- **Instant Price Display**: As soon as you change any setting (like selecting color instead of black & white), the app updates the exact price automatically. No hidden costs.
- **In-App Digital Wallet**:
  - Add money to your wallet using online payment methods.
  - Pay for orders with 1 tap.
  - If an order is ever canceled, your money is refunded back to your wallet instantly.
- **Live Order Progress & Notifications**:
  - Receive automatic mobile alerts when the shop starts printing, when it is finished, and when it is ready for collection.
  - View a digital **Pickup QR Code** in the app. Show this code to the shopkeeper for a quick, zero-contact collection.
- **Stationery Marketplace**: Browse and order notebooks, pens, paper reams, envelopes, or custom merchandise directly from shops.
- **Customer Support Desk**: Open a support request directly inside the app if you ever have a problem with an order or payment.

---

### For Print Shop Owners (Web Portal)

- **Live Print Queue (Digital Work Desk)**:
  - A live screen that alerts shopkeepers with a chime whenever a new order arrives.
  - Shows clear details: Customer name, exact document file, how many copies, paper type, color, binding instructions, and fulfillment choice.
- **One-Click Order Status Updates**:
  - Click a button to change order status: `Received` ➔ `Printing` ➔ `Ready for Pickup` ➔ `Completed`.
  - Customers automatically get notified on their phones when status changes.
- **Price Matrix Manager**:
  - Set your own custom prices per page (e.g., ₹2 per B&W page, ₹10 per Color page).
  - Set custom rates for different paper sizes and binding types (e.g., ₹20 for spiral binding).
- **Store Availability Switch**:
  - Toggle your store `Online` or `Offline` with one switch.
  - Set daily operating hours (Opening and Closing times).
- **Product Catalog Management**:
  - Add stationery products, add photos, prices, descriptions, and stock counts.
- **Earnings & Money Withdrawal**:
  - View total daily and monthly income, completed order counts, and available earnings balance.
  - Request to withdraw earnings directly to your bank account or UPI.

---

### For Platform Administrators (Admin Portal)

- **Shop Approvals & Onboarding**: Review new shop registration applications and approve legitimate print stores.
- **Payout Approvals**: Review shop withdrawal requests and safely transfer their earnings.
- **Global Support Desk**: Read customer or shop owner help requests, reply to messages, and issue refunds if necessary.

## 4. Step-by-Step How It Works (Everyday Scenarios)

### Scenario A: Ordering Document Prints

1. **Step 1: Pick a Shop** — Open the app, search for a nearby shop, and tap on it.
2. **Step 2: Upload File** — Choose your PDF file or document from your phone.
3. **Step 3: Customize Print Options** — Select B&W or Color, paper size, page numbers, copies, and binding style.
4. **Step 4: Check Cost & Pay** — Review the price breakdown and tap **Pay with Wallet**.
5. **Step 5: Real-Time Printing** — The shop gets an audio alert, prints your document according to your settings, and marks it as ready.
6. **Step 6: Pickup or Receive Delivery** — Walk into the shop, show your Pickup QR code on your phone screen, collect your printed documents, and walk out!

---

### Scenario B: Buying Stationery or Office Supplies

1. Open the app and head to the **Marketplace** tab.
2. Browse items like notebooks, pens, markers, or files listed by local shops.
3. Add items to your cart and proceed to checkout.
4. The shop receives your item order, packs it up, and makes it ready for pickup or delivery.

---

### Scenario C: How Shop Owners Manage Orders & Prices

1. **Managing Prices**: The shop owner logs into the Shop Web Portal and opens the **Pricing** tab. They type in their rates for single-sided B&W, double-sided B&W, color prints, and spiral binding. These rates are saved immediately.
2. **Handling Daily Print Jobs**: As orders roll in, the shop owner looks at the **Live Queue**. They see incoming documents, download the files with 1 click, print them on their machines, and click **Ready for Pickup**.

---

### Scenario D: How Payments, Refunds, and Withdrawals Work

1. **Adding Funds**: Customers top up their digital wallet in the app using credit/debit cards, UPI, or net banking.
2. **Instant Payment**: When an order is placed, money is deducted from the customer's wallet.
3. **Automatic Refund Guarantee**: If a shop cannot fulfill an order or if the customer cancels before printing starts, the full payment is instantly returned to the customer's wallet.
4. **Shop Earnings**: When an order is successfully completed, the shop's balance increases. The shop owner can click **Withdraw Funds** to receive their payout. An Admin verifies the request and releases the payment.

---

### Scenario E: How Support and Help Work

1. If a customer receives damaged prints or has a question about a payment deduction, they open the **Support** section in the app.
2. They select their order, write a description, and can upload a photo of the issue.
3. A support team member reads the message on the Admin Portal, communicates with the user or shop, and resolves the issue quickly (including issuing a refund if appropriate).
